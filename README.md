# cascaded-s2r.github.io

Project page of **Cascaded-S2R**, *Cascaded Sim-to-Real Tuning of a Fourth-Order Extended State Observer for Robot Manipulators*
(Hwarang Kim, Sesun You; manuscript under review, IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics, 2026).

Live page: https://cascaded-s2r.github.io (until 2026-11-18 the organization was BO-FO and the page bo-fo.github.io, after the earlier title of the manuscript)

Static site, no build step (GitHub Pages serves the `main` branch root):

- `index.html`, `styles.css`, `site.js`, the page; `site.js` only drives the featured-video picker and the looping clips.
- `assets/`, the study-design figure (`fig1_pipeline`, the manuscript's Fig. 1 without equation numbers) and poster frames (`assets/posters/`).
- `videos/`, the silent overview `teaser.mp4` (1:12 since 2026-11-18, the featured video, autoplaying muted in a loop: the poster, a four-box schematic of the cascade with live sim and robot clips, the loaded y line, the unloaded z line with the joint-5 torque, a 3 x 3 clip grid of the straight lines, the y wave line, a 2 x 3 grid of the wave lines; `video/make_teaser.py` of the paper repository; thumbnail `assets/posters/teaser.jpg`), the narrated supplementary video (`supp_video.mp4`, 4:28 since 2026-11-18, opening on the poster image while the title is read once, the title card dropped because the poster carries the title, the narration of the overview, the set-up and the summary re-synthesized for the cascaded sim-to-real framing of the manuscript), its seven slide segments with narration (`s1_overview` … `s6_summary`, `s5b_ablation` the wave-line ablation), and `videos/loops/`, one looping clip for each hardware setting of Table II plus the rejected ρ = 104 (the ρ = 87 clip was removed on 2026-11-08) (whole recorded arm, y, z and end-on x lines, one cycle, measured deviation drawn ×100; rendered by `video/make_page_loops.py` of the paper repository); `videos/motions_grid.mp4`, the eleven validation motions in Isaac Sim, production ADRC vs a damping-bound optimum of an earlier revision (`scripts/record_motions.py` + `video/make_motion_grid.py`).
